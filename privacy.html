Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Who we are

AYN Limited ("AYN", "we", "us", "our") is a business utility brokerage. We help businesses compare, switch and renew their energy and water contracts with suppliers. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you use our website at aynlimited.site or ask us for a quote.

AYN Limited is the controller of your personal data for the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can contact us about this policy or your data at any time:

AYN Limited
Email: info@aynlimited.site
Phone: 020 8135 7655
Hours: Mon–Sat, 9am–5pm

2. What information we collect

We only collect what we need to provide our service. This may include:

  • Contact details — your name, business name, job title, email address and phone number.
  • Business and utility information — your current supplier, meter details (such as MPAN/MPRN or supply numbers), usage, contract end dates and recent bills you choose to share with us.
  • Communications — records of emails, calls and messages between us, including any authority you give us to act on your behalf.
  • Website data — basic technical information such as your browser, device and pages visited, if analytics is enabled on our site.

Most of our clients are businesses. Where you are a sole trader or partnership, some business information may also be personal data, and we treat it with the same care.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare quotes from our supplier partners.
  • To arrange a switch or renewal you have asked us to handle, and to manage your account during your contract.
  • To contact you about upcoming contract renewals and relevant options for your business.
  • To keep records and meet our legal, regulatory and accounting obligations.
  • To improve our website and service.

4. Our lawful bases

We process your information on the following lawful bases:

  • Contract — where processing is needed to provide the service you have asked for, such as preparing a quote or arranging a switch.
  • Legitimate interests — to run and improve our business and to contact business clients about renewals and similar services, where this does not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records required by law.
  • Consent — where we ask for it, for example for certain marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share your information only where needed to provide our service, including with:

  • Energy and water suppliers on our panel, so they can provide quotes and set up contracts.
  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as email, website hosting and IT providers, under appropriate data protection terms.
  • Credit reference agencies, where a supplier needs to carry out a credit check before offering a contract.
  • Regulators, ombudsmen, or law enforcement, where we are legally required to.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers may store data outside the UK. Where this happens, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contract clauses.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we are working with you and for up to six years after our relationship ends, so we can deal with any queries or claims and meet our record-keeping obligations. Enquiries that do not lead to a contract are deleted after 24 months unless you ask us to keep in touch. After that, information is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Keeping your data secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and we only give access to people who need it.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • object to or restrict how we use your data, including for direct marketing;
  • ask for your data to be transferred to you or another organisation;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email info@aynlimited.site. We will respond within one month.

10. Cookies

Our website uses only the cookies needed for it to work. If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated shown at the top.
